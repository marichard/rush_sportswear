## Project Overview

The VP of US Sales tasked the analytics team with evaluating raw sales data.
- Identify top-performing product categories and geographic performance across Men's and Women's product lines.
- Evaluate retail partner purchasing volume.

## Business Questions & Findings

### 1. Highest Selling Product Category (2021)
- **Top Category:** Men's Street Footwear
- **Total Revenue:** **$23,294,836.00**

### 2. Top State for Women's Products Sales (2021)
- **Top State:** Maine
- **Total Revenue:** **$2,176,301.00**

### 3. Top State for Men's Products Sales (2021)
- **Top State:** Delaware
- **Total Revenue:** **$2,334,300.00**

### 4. Top Retailer by Units Purchased
- **2021 Top Retailer:** Foot Locker (**1,097,410 units**)
- **2020 Top Retailer:** Amazon (**317,930 units**)
